Good to know

A few house rules from the venue, so the day goes smoothly for everyone.

  • Party hours: the reception runs from 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
  • Your RSVP is your seat: please come with only the guests in your reply. We send the final headcount to the venue on November 4, and they can’t add people after that.

  • Outside food and drinks: please don’t bring any. The venue charges a fee for food from outside.
  • Non-smoking venue: smoking is only allowed in the venue’s designated area.
  • Little ones: please keep an eye on them. The venue charges for anything broken.
